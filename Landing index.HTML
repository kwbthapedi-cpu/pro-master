THE PLATFORM

Your instrument.
Your label.

Pro M.A.S.T.E.R is built for artists who create. You bring the voice, the vision, the feeling — the platform gives you the studio tools and the label infrastructure to release it to the world. You are the artist. The AI is your instrument.

  • Voice to Instrument — hum a melody, choose Grand Piano, Kora, Sax, or Strings. Your voice becomes the instrument in real time. No musical training needed.
  • Billy Black AI Producer — your in-session AI producer knows your key, BPM, and recorded clips. He gives live feedback, chord suggestions, and mix tips.
  • Full DAW in the browser — multi-track recording, FX chains, 16-pad drums, MIDI support. Everything runs in your browser. No downloads, no plugins.
  • Pro M.A.S.T.E.R Record Label — every artist on the platform is part of a living catalogue. Pro and Member artists distribute to Spotify and Apple Music through the label.
THE STUDIO

Everything you need
to make a record.

01
🎤
Voice Engine
Real-time pitch detection using the YIN autocorrelation algorithm. Sing or hum and your voice is instantly mapped to any instrument — piano, bass, saxophone, kora, strings. Three modes: Melody, Bass, Beatbox.
02
🎹
15 Instruments
Grand Piano, Electric Piano, Hammond Organ, Harpsichord, Upright Bass, Synth Bass, Alto Sax, Trumpet, Flute, Strings, Warm Pad — and the MagniProjekta signature instruments: Kora, Mbira, Oud. All synthesized, all live.
03
🥁
16 Drum Pads
Kick, snare, hi-hat, open hat, clap, tom, rim, crash, shaker, cowbell, claves, tamb, and more — all synthesized from scratch using the Web Audio API. No samples, no latency, no limits.
04
🤖
Billy Black AI
Your resident AI producer powered by the Anthropic API. Billy Black knows your session state — key, BPM, instruments, clips recorded. He auto-analyzes every take and responds like a real producer in the room with you.
05
🎛️
MIDI + FX Chain
Connect any MIDI controller via the Web MIDI API — velocity sensitive, mod wheel mapped. FX chain includes Reverb, Delay, Compressor, EQ, and Chorus — all running natively in the browser's audio graph.
06
📼
Multi-track DAW
Record unlimited tracks in-platform. Clips appear on the shared timeline with waveform visualization and colour-coded instrument lanes. Export stems as WAV when your subscription allows. Project cloud save coming Phase 4.
